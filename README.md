# RA3UltimateWar
Red Alert 3: Ultimate War is an RA3 mod based on Remix 0.3.7 by KnightVVV. The mod primarily makes balance adjustments and vanilla reversions. Most credit goes to KnightVVV for all the base files.

Official Remix 0.3.7 page:
https://www.nexusmods.com/commandandconquerredalert3/mods/2

Support KnightVVV:
https://www.patreon.com/knightvvv


## Changes from Remix 0.3.7

### Allies
- **Century Bomber (unit):** Restored vanilla ability to carry and eject infantry (non-Strato upgraded only).
- **Dog House (structure):** Decreased build cost from 500 to 250.
- **Ion Cannon (protocol):** Increased reload time from 480 seconds to 510 seconds.
- **Javelin Soldier (unit):** Restored vanilla Laser-Guided Mode ability (was broken).
- **Multigunner Turret (structure):** Restored vanilla infantry garrison system.
- **Proton Collider (structure):** Increased damage from 400 to 500 and damage taper-off from 200 to 250, as well as made it able to damage aerial units.
- **Zeus Warship Authorization (protocol):** Decreased reload time from 420 seconds to 300 seconds. 
- **Zeus Warship (unit):** Increased build time from 60 seconds to 120 seconds.
- **Warren (skirmish AI personality):** Restored vanilla behavior of not selling off outposts (doesn't make sense strategically) and made them upgrade outpost tech levels.
- **Giles (skirmish AI personality):** Restored vanilla behavior of not selling off outposts (doesn't make sense strategically) and made them upgrade outpost tech levels.
- **Lisette (skirmish AI personality):** Restored vanilla behavior of not selling off outposts (doesn't make sense strategically) and made them upgrade outpost tech levels.

### Empire
- **Balloon Bombs (protocol):** Increased normal damage from 250 to 350 and enhanced kamikaze damage from 500 to 700.
- **Final Squadron (protocol):** Increased aircraft gun damage from 25 to 100, normal kamikaze damage from 500 to 600, and enhanced kamikaze damage from 750 to 900.
- **Giga Fortress Authorization (protocol):** Increased build cost from 0 to 7000 and decreased reload time from 360 seconds to 300 seconds. 
- **Giga Fortress (unit):** Increased egg build time from 40 seconds to 100 seconds (egg unpacking time 20 seconds still).

### Soviet
- **Orbital Downpour (protocol):** Decreased all random debris damage by 50% (main debris damage unchanged) and increased reload time from 420 seconds to 510 seconds.
- **Steel Fortress Authorization (protocol):** Increased build cost from 6000 to 7000 and decreased reload time from 420 seconds to 300 seconds. 
- **Steel Fortress (unit):** Increased build time from 60 seconds to 120 seconds.
