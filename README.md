# RA3UltimateWar
Red Alert 3: Ultimate War is an RA3 mod based on Remix 0.3.7 by KnightVVV. The mod primarily makes balance adjustments and vanilla reversions. Almost all credit goes to KnightVVV.

Official Remix 0.3.7 page:
https://www.nexusmods.com/commandandconquerredalert3/mods/2


## Changes from Remix 0.3.7
### Allies
- **Century Bomber:** Restored vanilla ability to carry and eject infantry.
- **Dog House:** Decreased cost from 500 to 250.
- **Javelin Soldier:** Restored vanilla Laser-Guided Mode ability (was broken).
- **Multigunner Turret:** Restored vanilla infantry garrison system.
- **Proton Collider:** Increased damage from 400 to 600 and damage taper-off from 200 to 300.
